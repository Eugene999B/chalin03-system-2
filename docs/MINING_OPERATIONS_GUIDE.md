# Chalin 03 Mining Operations User Guide

Mining Operations is independent from Spare Parts and Equipment Hire. Mining
workers login to Mining, select an authorized site and see only Mining users,
permissions, workers, Fleet context and operational records.

## Controlled workflow

1. Administrator creates sites and assigns staff/site access.
2. Supervisor opens the daily/shift log and records crew, conditions and notes.
3. Production, stockpiles, dispatch and equipment hours are recorded against the
   correct site, date, shift, material and unit.
4. Fuel receipts, issues, tank balances and reconciliations are recorded with
   asset and meter/shift evidence.
5. Expenses, contractors and incidents are recorded and approved independently.
6. Site closing reconciles production, stockpiles, dispatch, fuel, expenses and
   incidents before approval.
7. Mining reports and documents are filtered by the selected site and period.
8. Mining worker profiles, documents and permissions remain inside Mining.

Direct links to another category are blocked. Conflicting existing assignments
are preserved for Safe Conflict Review by the original System Administrator.
