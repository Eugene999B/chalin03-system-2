# Chalin 03 Equipment Hire User Guide

Equipment Hire is independent from Spare Parts and Mining. Hire workers login to
Equipment Hire, select an authorized base/yard/location and see only Hire users,
permissions, workers, Fleet context and commercial records.

## Commercial and operational workflow

1. Administrator creates Hire locations and staff access.
2. Create/search the customer and record the equipment enquiry.
3. Check availability and approved rate cards before preparing a quotation.
4. Complete required approval before creating a contract.
5. Assign and dispatch available equipment with meter and condition evidence.
6. Record customer-approved work logs and billable hours.
7. Create invoices, record deposits/payments and issue receipts.
8. Complete return inspection, damage assessment and equipment release.
9. Review aging, utilization, outstanding balances, documents and Activity Log.
10. Hire worker profiles and permissions remain inside Equipment Hire.

The Hire layout uses real icons rather than escaped Unicode text. Direct links to
another category are blocked, and ambiguous legacy assignments require Safe
Conflict Review by the original System Administrator.
