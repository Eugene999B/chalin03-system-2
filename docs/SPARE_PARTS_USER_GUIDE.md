# Chalin 03 Spare Parts User Guide

This guide applies only to the Spare Parts business and its two stores. Mining
sites and Equipment Hire locations are separate businesses with separate users,
permissions and worker profiles.

## Daily operating sequence

1. Login to **Spare Parts** and choose the correct store.
2. Confirm products, stock and customer details before a sale.
3. Record the real Cash, MoMo, Bank and Other payment allocation.
4. For installments, review deposit, schedule, guarantor/reference and delivery
   policy before activation.
5. Print or send the final receipt. The store **Business Phone** is printed as
   the receipt telephone and MoMo number.
6. Record purchases, supplier payments, expenses, debts and returns through
   their dedicated pages; never rewrite an old transaction to hide an error.
7. Complete Daily Closing with independently counted channels and explain every
   shortage or excess.
8. Review reports, Activity Log, Security Centre and Full System Backup status.

## Category boundary

Spare Parts worker profiles, permissions, stores, receipts, sales, stock,
installments, debts, purchases and reports cannot be opened by Mining or Hire
worker accounts. Only the original System Administrator may switch across all
three categories.
